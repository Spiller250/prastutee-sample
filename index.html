Who we are

Prastutee began in 1997 as a group of social activists working with poor and marginalised people. Today it works across undivided Koraput district and, more recently, the coastal area of Puri.

  1. 1997Started as a group of social activists
  2. 2000Registered as an organisation
  3. 2005FCRA registration

Vision

A just, eco-friendly society where everyone has basic and fundamental rights.

Mission

Participatory development that values gender equity, built in partnership with the people and institutions involved.

How we work

We build and strengthen networks with stakeholder institutions, including government agencies and public bodies.
